# FitFriend: competitors and what they sell (started 2026-11-03)

Sources: screenshots the owner took of the apps on his iPhone on 2026-11-03 (marked "owner screenshot"), plus web search results
(marked with the page). Prices change; check the store listing before relying on a number. **Identity verification at competitors
is NOT verified yet**: the searches said nothing about it either way, so it must not be claimed as our advantage until checked in each app.

**Gymly, seen from the inside (owner screenshots, 2026-11-03):** a grid of member cards (username, age, distance, a "level" number), tabs All, Female, Male, Actives (green or red dot for online), filters for distance (up to 1000 km) and age (up to 70), a "Be the most visible!" rocket card (a visibility boost, shown with an eye counter; price not seen), a camera for Post and Story, and a feed grid of photos. Sign-up: username (15 characters), profile picture, **the gym you attend (picked from a list; the owner saw an Israeli gym in Modi'in)**, gender (male or female only), weight and height, and the best squat, deadlift and bench press, which give the level. The nearest members the owner saw were **81 km to over 3,800 km away**: the app is nearly empty in Israel. Many profile photos are shirtless mirror selfies (a dating-style feel); no identity check was visible. Paid plan from the App Store listing: weekly, monthly and yearly subscription (see the table).

**SpotYourMatch ("SYM"), seen from the inside (owner screenshots, 2026-11-03):** a swipe app (Tinder style: skip, like, undo), tabs Home, Explore (a community feed of posts such as "looking for a lifting buddy"), Interactions (Match, Viewed Me, Liked Me), Chats and Profile. A banner ad sits at the bottom of every screen. Many profiles have age 0, no photo or a famous person's name; members are thousands of miles away (2,350 and 3,178 miles). Plans (currency not shown, probably USD): **Plus** $3.99 per month or $49.99 per year (200 likes a day, Passport mode to change city, 1 boost a month, no ads); **Gold** $9.99 or $119 (everything in Plus, see who liked you, daily top picks, unlimited likes, 2 boosts, 5 super invites a day), marked "Most popular"; **Platinum** $14.99 or $179 (everything in Gold, priority likes, message before matching, nearby people on the map, higher ranking, 4 boosts). The "Save 20%" label on the yearly plans does not match the numbers (12 months of Gold is 119.88, the yearly price is 119; Plus yearly is even $2 more than 12 months).

Lesson from Gymly: **density decides everything** (81 km to the nearest member means the app feels dead); the gym is a natural community to start from (pick your gym at sign-up); a numeric "level" from the big lifts is a simple way to match people; a boost for visibility is a second thing they can sell besides the subscription.

## Adjacent (tracking and community, not matching)
Strava (about $11.99 per month or $79.99 per year), Hevy Pro ($2.99 per month), Strong, Fitbod (about $16 per month).

## What this tells us
1. **Price anchor in Israel: ₪7.90 per month, ₪59.90 per year.** A first FitFriend Plus price near **₪9.90 to ₪12.90 per month** is realistic; more only with clear extra value.
2. What gets locked behind the paywall elsewhere: direct messages to anyone, recurring activities, hosting several activities, advanced filters. Never charge for safety (identity check, block, report).
3. The big risk of the whole category is **density**: apps feel empty when few members live nearby. Start with existing communities (a running group, a gym, a campus).
4. Our possible differences (to be verified, not assumed): 1:1 partner plus the shared streak, the real identity check, real tracking (steps, runs) in the same app.
5. Ideas worth adopting: a sport search on the "what and when" step, duration chips, a level range, a place picker on a map with search, cost splitting.
6. Do not adopt: background location (hard in Apple review and in privacy), third-party sign-in until later.
