## Adjacent (tracking and community, not matching)
Strava (about $11.99 per month or $79.99 per year), Hevy Pro ($2.99 per month), Strong, Fitbod (about $16 per month).

## What this tells us
1. **Price anchor in Israel: ₪7.90 per month, ₪59.90 per year.** A first FitFriend Plus price near **₪9.90 to ₪12.90 per month** is realistic; more only with clear extra value.
2. What gets locked behind the paywall elsewhere: direct messages to anyone, recurring activities, hosting several activities, advanced filters. Never charge for safety (identity check, block, report).
3. The big risk of the whole category is **density**: apps feel empty when few members live nearby. Start with existing communities (a running group, a gym, a campus).
4. Our possible differences (to be verified, not assumed): 1:1 partner plus the shared streak, the real identity check, real tracking (steps, runs) in the same app.
5. Ideas worth adopting: a sport search on the "what and when" step, duration chips, a level range, a place picker on a map with search, cost splitting.
6. Do not adopt: background location (hard in Apple review and in privacy), third-party sign-in until later.
